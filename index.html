1. You're dealing directly with each other

Listings must be accurate: real photos, a fair description, and a price you'll actually honour. Don't list anything illegal, stolen, counterfeit, or unsafe.

Prefer public places for handovers, verify items before paying, and trust your judgement. LinkHub can't guarantee the identity or intentions of anyone you contact here.
